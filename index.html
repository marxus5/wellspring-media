Our Philosophy

Craft lives in the details no one notices.

Post production is invisible when it's done right — and that's exactly how we like it. At Wellspring, our work serves the story, never the spectacle. We obsess over the gradients of shadow, the weight of silence, the precise moment a cut lands.

We're a new company, but our team brings decades of combined experience from some of the most respected facilities in the industry. We built Wellspring to do things differently — smaller, more intentional, and always craft-first.

What We Do

Full-spectrum post production

01

Color Grading & Finishing

From naturalistic correction to stylized looks, our colorists work in DaVinci Resolve on a calibrated DCI-P3 suite. We deliver for cinema, streaming, broadcast, and web.

02

Offline & Online Editing

Whether you need a skilled offline editor to shape your story or a precision online conform and QC, we provide end-to-end picture editorial across all formats.

03

Sound Design & Mixing

Our Dolby-certified mixing stage handles everything from intimate dialogue work to full cinematic sound design. We mix for Atmos, 5.1, and stereo with equal care.

04

Motion Graphics & Titles

Bespoke title sequences, lower thirds, end cards, and animated graphics designed to live perfectly within your visual world — built from scratch, never from a template.

05

VFX & Compositing

Clean-up, wire removal, set extensions, titling, and complex compositing work. We bring practical and digital elements together seamlessly in Nuke and After Effects.

06

Deliverables & Mastering

DCP creation, streaming deliverables, broadcast masters, archival formats. We know the technical requirements of every major platform and fulfillment pipeline.
